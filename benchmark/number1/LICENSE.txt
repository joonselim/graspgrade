It is derived from the Ego-Tactile Manipulation dataset by OpenGraph Labs
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenGraphLabs-Research/ego-tactile-manipulation),
CC BY 4.0. Changes: contact segments were computed from the glove signal
(sum of 80 sensors over 800), and a cleaned version joins gaps of 3 frames
or less and drops pieces shorter than 3 frames. No sensor values are included.
